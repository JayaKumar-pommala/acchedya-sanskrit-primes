# Source and redistribution notes

This bundle combines code, original translations and notes, and transcriptions or references to works with different provenance and usage terms. **Do not assume one license covers the whole repository.**

- The dossier identifies source keys, editions, repositories, and some source-specific terms in §16 of `SANSKRIT_VERSES.md`.
- The Gaṇita Kaumudī Part II scan is a 1942 printed edition. The Sanskrit readings in this bundle come from OCR and project restorations and have not yet been collated line by line against the page images.
- The dossier notes that the cited vishvAsa source texts are CC BY 4.0 and that GRETIL materials are for research use. Verify the terms that apply to the exact files before redistributing their transcriptions.
- Ancient source works may be public domain while a modern edition, transcription, encoding, or translation has separate terms.
- The working translations in the dossier are project translations; check and document their authorship before assigning a repository-wide license.

Until the provenance and terms of every included text are checked, leave the repository without a blanket license or add a license only for clearly identified original code and commentary, with exclusions stated explicitly.
